I made my logo using [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/). I spent about two days to design and made the logo, in which some design ingenuity and symbolism are incorporated.

# Concept behind the Work

I'm a amateur lover of Chinese calligraphy. The logo is a Chinese word 志, meaning *aspiration, ambition*, or *will*, which is also the last character of my given name: 晨志. The style is [seal script (篆書)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_script), a very ancient calligraphy style that has been existing for over 2000 years.

The top part of the logo is designed to mimic the Greek letter \\ \Psi \\, which is usually used to represent the wave function in quantum mechanics, describing the state of a system.
