I made my logo using [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/). It took me about two days to design and make the logo, in which some design ingenuity and symbolism are incorporated.

# Concept behind the Work

I'm a amateur lover of Chinese calligraphy. The logo is a Chinese word 志, meaning *aspiration, ambition*, or *will*, which is also the last character of my given name: 晨志. The style is [seal script (篆書)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_script), a very ancient calligraphy style that has been existing for over 2000 years.

The upper portion of the character is designed to mimic the Greek letter \\( \Psi \\), which is usually used to represent the wave function in quantum mechanics that describes the state of a system. In the meantime, the lower portion of 志 is 心, meaning *heart* in Chinese. Another fun fact of quantum mechanics is that we need to *measure* the state to get physically meaningful information about the state. Together, the logo delivers a spirit: *observing the universe using my heart*. I hope this logo can reflect my passion and love in physics and express this information to those who are interested in my research and career.
